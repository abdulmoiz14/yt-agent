# Stage: research

Goal: understand the topic well enough to choose an angle. This is NOT source verification and
NOT fact-checking - those are separate stages with separate passes.

Precondition: `web_research` is `available` in `project.json`. If it is `unavailable` or
`unverified`, do not research from memory and present it as research. Mark the stage
`needs_input` and ask the creator for sources (links, books, PDFs) or permission to proceed from
general knowledge clearly labelled as unverified leads.

## Do

1. Search broadly with WebSearch, then WebFetch the pages you will actually rely on. Prefer
   primary sources (contemporary accounts, archives, official records), then scholarship
   (university presses, peer-reviewed, academic encyclopedias), then reputable secondary
   (major museums, established publications). Note forums, content farms and AI summaries as weak.
2. For a history topic, look specifically for: where accounts disagree, which "famous facts" are
   later legend, dates that differ between calendars or sources, numbers that are estimates.
3. Write `research/notes.md`:
   - **The question** the video answers, in one sentence.
   - **Three candidate angles**, each with why it would hold attention and what it needs to be true.
   - **What is solid** - with the URL you actually fetched for each point.
   - **What is disputed or legend** - who says what.
   - **Open questions** you could not resolve.
   - **Leads** - pages found but not fetched, marked `NOT FETCHED`.
4. Optional niche check: `/yt-viral` only if `niche_research` is available or the creator hands
   you a list. Present multiples as the tool reports them; never estimate view counts.

## Never

- Write a URL you did not get from a search result or fetch in this session, or that the creator gave you.
- Put a quote in quotation marks unless you copied it from a fetched page.
- Fill a gap with a plausible date, number or name. Write it as an open question.

## Checkpoint

Show the three angles and your recommendation. Wait for the creator to pick or redirect.
Record their choice at the top of `research/notes.md`, then mark the stage done.
