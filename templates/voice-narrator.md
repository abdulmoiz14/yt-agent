# voice.md - faceless narrator version

For a channel with no face and a synthetic or hired narrator. Copy what fits into
`~/.claude/youtube/voice.md` (or replace it) and fill it in. Every skill reads this file; for a
narrated channel it defines the NARRATOR, not you on camera.

## Who I am talking to

One person, named properly. Not "history fans" but "someone who half-remembers Caesar from school,
watches on their phone at night, and wants the story, not a lecture".

## How the narrator talks

Paste two or three paragraphs of narration you would be proud of - yours, or written in the style you
want. Then the rules, concretely:
- Sentence length (e.g. "mostly under 20 words, one long sentence per paragraph at most").
- Tense and person (e.g. "past tense, third person; 'we' only for 'we know' / 'we don't know'").
- Tone (e.g. "calm, dry, a little wry; never breathless").
- How uncertainty is said: "the sources say", "according to legend", "probably" - and when.

## Words the narrator never uses

The filler of the genre. Be specific: "dive in", "let's get into it", "buckle up", "shocking",
"you won't believe", "little did they know", "throughout history", "fast forward".

## Words the narrator does use

The few phrases that make the channel sound like itself. Keep the list short.

## What the narrator will not claim

- No number without a source in the fact-check ledger; estimates are said as estimates.
- No quote unless it is in the sources, attributed by name.
- Legend is labelled as legend. Contested points are said to be contested.
- Images are illustrations - never described as "a photo of" or "a painting from the time" unless
  they really are a cleared historical artwork.

## My format

- Narrated, faceless; voice: (engine and voice, e.g. "Kokoro bm_george, speed 0.95, pitch 1.0").
- Length (e.g. "10-12 minutes"); a date/chapter title card every 2-3 minutes.
- Visual style preset (natural | comic | roundhead | stickman) and any recurring characters.
- Opening: the hook is the first line - no channel intro, no "in this video".
- Close: one ask, at the end, never in the first minute.
- Music bed yes/no (only tracks you have the rights to); transition sound yes/no.
